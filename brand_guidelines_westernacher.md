# Westernacher Consulting Brand Guidelines

## Company Overview

**Founded:** 1969, Karlsruhe, Germany
**Headquarters:** Heidelberg, Germany
**Employees:** 1,100+ consultants
**Presence:** 33 cities across 26 countries (Europe, Asia, North America, South America)
**Ownership:** 100% employee-owned
**Sustainability:** Carbon neutral since 2021
**SAP Partnership:** 30+ years

Westernacher Consulting is a global leader in business and SAP consulting, serving medium to large enterprises seeking sustainable growth and digital transformation. With 55+ years of innovation, the firm is recognized as a pioneer in implementing SAP technologies including S/4HANA, EWM, TM, Yard Logistics, and Analytics platforms.

## Visual Style

### Design Principles
- **Clean minimalism** with generous whitespace
- **Smooth transitions** (0.3s standard timing)
- **Rounded corners** (10-15px border-radius for cards, 6px for inputs)
- **Gradient overlays** on imagery and featured elements
- **Icon-driven** navigation and interaction patterns

### Component Styling

**Cards & Panels:**
- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `1px solid #E2E6EA`
- Border radius: `10px`
- Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`
- Hover shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`

**Buttons:**
- Primary: `#1596D1` background, white text, 6px radius
- Hover: `#1178A8` with subtle lift (`translateY(-1px)`)
- Transition: `150ms ease`

**Interactive Elements:**
- Hover transitions with color shifts
- Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px #E8F4FB`

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Core Attributes
- **Innovative & Entrepreneurial:** Inquisitive, creative thinkers with a strong entrepreneurial spirit
- **Quality-Focused & Results-Oriented:** Committed to breaking new ground with measurable outcomes
- **Collaborative & Partnership-Driven:** Emphasizes teamwork, co-creation, and customer success
- **Pioneer Mentality:** First-mover approach to technology adoption and implementation

### Writing Guidelines
- Use confident, forward-looking language
- Emphasize partnership and collaboration over hierarchy
- Lead with innovation and transformation outcomes
- Be specific about expertise (SAP, digital transformation, operational excellence)
- Maintain professional authority while remaining approachable
- Avoid generic corporate jargon; favor clear, purposeful language

### Key Phrases & Vocabulary
- "Nonstop Innovation"
- "Believe in partnership"
- "Operational excellence"
- "Digital transformation"
- "Technology-driven innovation"
- "Sustainable growth"
- "Pioneer" / "Pioneering"

---

## Logo Usage

### Logotype
- Gray-toned wordmark: "westernacher"
- Always accompanied by "Nonstop Innovation" tagline
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "w" character

### Placement
- Top-left in navigation and headers
- Centered in hero sections when applicable
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolor outside approved palette

## Application to LinkedIn Post Generator

When generating posts under the Westernacher brand:

1. **Visual Identity:** Apply Westernacher Blue (`#1596D1`) as the primary interactive color. Use the brand gradient for hero/feature elements. Maintain clean, minimal design with generous whitespace.

2. **Content Voice:** Posts should reflect the innovative, partnership-driven tone. Reference digital transformation, SAP expertise, and operational excellence where relevant. Use confident, forward-looking language.

3. **Topics Alignment:** Prioritize topics around Innovation, Digital Transformation, SAP & Technology, Supply Chain & Logistics, Sustainability, and Leadership.

4. **Hashtags:** Include `#WesternacherConsulting` and `#NonstopInnovation` alongside topic-relevant tags.
